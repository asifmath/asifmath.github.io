Teaching Approach

My teaching aims to combine clear structure with conceptual understanding. I focus on presenting mathematical ideas in a way that is both rigorous and accessible, especially for students from non-mathematics backgrounds.

I place particular emphasis on:
  • structured problem-solving
  • tutorials and guided practice
  • supporting students in the early stages of their studies

I have also been involved in activities to promote student engagement in mathematics, including organizing an annual outreach event (Mathematrix) with interactive components.

Additional Interests: I am interested in connecting mathematical theory with applications in areas such as quantitative finance and data-driven modeling, and in developing teaching formats that support interdisciplinary learning.
